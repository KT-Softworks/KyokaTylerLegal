# Privacy Policy for Add text to lock screen.

**Effective Date:** September 14, 2025

Thank you for using "Add text to lock screen." ("the App"). Your privacy is important to us. This privacy policy explains how your information is handled within the App.

## Data Collection

**We collect no personal data.**

"Add text to lock screen." is a simple utility designed to function entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any personal or non-personal information from you or your device.

## Data Usage and Storage

The only data the App handles is the text and font choice you enter. This data is used for the sole purpose of displaying your custom text on your device's Lock Screen widget.

This information is stored **locally on your device** in a secure container shared only between the main application and its widget extension. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or any third party. The "History" feature also stores your previously used text strings locally on your device for your convenience.

## Data Sharing

We do not share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data to begin with.

## Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. The app is designed for a general audience and does not contain any content unsuitable for children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
