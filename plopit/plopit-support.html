Frequently Asked Questions

Q: I bought the Full Game but it locked again! How do I get it back?
A: Don't worry, you never have to pay twice! Tap the golden Menu button in the bottom right corner of the game. On the Settings page, tap the gray "Restore Purchases" button. Enter your Apple ID password if asked, and the game will instantly unlock.

Q: There is no sound when I pop a bubble!
A: Make sure your iPhone or iPad is not on Silent Mode (check the switch on the side of your phone, or the bell icon in your Control Center), and turn your media volume up.
