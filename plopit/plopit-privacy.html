Privacy Policy for Plop-It!

Last Updated: April 2026

1. We Collect No Data

Plop-It! was designed for kids, which means privacy is our top priority. We do not collect, store, track, or share any personal information, location data, or usage analytics. Everything you do in the app stays entirely on your own physical device.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Because our app does not transmit any data to the internet or any external servers, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13, because we do not collect information from anyone.

3. In-App Purchases

Plop-It! offers a single optional In-App Purchase to unlock the full game. All payments are processed securely by Apple's App Store. We do not process these payments, and we never see, collect, or have access to your credit card or financial information.

4. Apple Standard EULA

Your use of Plop-It! is governed by the standard Apple End User License Agreement (EULA), which you agree to when downloading the app from the App Store.
